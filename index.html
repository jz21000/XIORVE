Cycloidal / RV Drive

Heavy-Load High-Rigidity Transmission

Designed for high impact resistance and severe duty cycles. Provides robust torsional stiffness for heavy-duty industrial robots.

  • Superior torsional rigidity for heavy payload handling
  • Stable speed reduction with low vibration signature
